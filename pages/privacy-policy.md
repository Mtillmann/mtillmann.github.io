## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Martin's Blog. This website is committed to protecting your privacy and complying with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This Privacy Policy explains how this website collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you visit my blog.

This blog is a purely static website hosted by an external provider and delivered as static files. It runs no server-side scripts, databases, or account systems. When you visit the blog, the hosting provider writes Apache access logs for technical operation and security purposes. According to the provider, your IP address is anonymized in these logs, so the logs cannot be traced back to you. The blog itself only loads Google Fonts and Google Analytics if you explicitly opt in via the consent dialog, and the Utterances commenting system only if you actively choose to load it.

Please take a moment to read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand how this website handles your personal information.

## 2. Information This Website Collects

This website only processes data if you explicitly opt in or provide it voluntarily. Here's how this website collects and uses your data:

**a. Server Logs:** This static website is served by an Apache web server operated by an external hosting provider. When you visit the blog, the provider automatically writes server access logs that contain technical data such as your browser type, the pages you view, and the date and time of your visit. Your IP address is anonymized by the provider before it is stored in these logs. As the operator of the blog, I have no access to these logs.

**b. Personal Information:** This website only collects personal information, such as your name and email address, if you voluntarily provide it, for example when you contact me via email. The blog has no registration or contact forms.

**c. Usage Information:** Google Fonts and Google Analytics may collect usage information such as your IP address and usage data, but only if you explicitly opt in via the consent dialog. Without your consent, these services are not loaded at all.

## 3. Use of Personal Information

This website uses the personal information you provide voluntarily or with your explicit consent for the following purposes:

- To respond to your inquiries or comments.
- To improve and personalize your experience on this blog.
- To notify you about blog updates or relevant content, with your explicit consent.

## 4. Third-Party Services

a. **Google Fonts:** This website uses Google Fonts to enhance the design and readability of the blog. Google Fonts is only loaded if you explicitly opt in via the consent dialog. Google may process usage data as described in their [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

b. **Google Analytics:** This website only loads Google Analytics if you explicitly opt in via the consent dialog. Google Analytics may collect usage data, which is subject to Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can also opt out of Google Analytics using the [Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

c. **Utterances:** This website uses the Utterances commenting system, but it will only load if you choose to activate it. The Utterances commenting system may collect data as described in their [Privacy Policy](https://utteranc.es/privacy).

## 5. Data Retention

This website does not itself store any personal data. Because the blog is static and I, as the operator, have no access to the hosting provider's anonymized server logs, there is no personal data on this website that I could retain. Personal data you voluntarily provide to me (for example via email) is kept only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or as required by law.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the following rights under GDPR:

- The right to access your personal information. *(There is no personal data stored on this website.)*
- The right to rectify inaccurate personal information. *(Correction would only apply to data you sent me directly.)*
- The right to erasure of personal information (subject to legal limitations). *(Nothing is stored on this website; anonymized logs cannot be linked to you.)*
- The right to restrict processing of personal information. *(This website does not process personal data.)*
- The right to data portability. *(There is no personal data on this website to export.)*
- The right to object to processing of personal information. *(This website does not process personal data.)*
- The right to withdraw consent (if applicable) at any time. *(You can do so anytime via the shield icon in the navigation.)*

In practice, since this static website stores no personal data of yours, these rights only matter for the personal information you voluntarily send me directly.

You can exercise these rights by contacting this website using the information provided in Section 9.

## 7. Security

This website takes reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. However, please be aware that no data transmission over the internet is entirely secure, and this website cannot guarantee the security of your information.

## 8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This website reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in its practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The revised Privacy Policy will be posted on this blog, and the date of the latest update will be indicated at the top of the page.

## 9. Contact This Website

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests related to this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact me using the contact details in the [Imprint](/pages/imprint.html).

## 10. Consent

This website does not process any personal data without your active, explicit consent. When you visit the blog for the first time, a consent dialog asks you to choose whether Google Fonts and Google Analytics may be loaded. Your choice is stored locally in your browser's localStorage and can be changed at any time by clicking the shield icon in the navigation. Nothing is sent to Google or any other third party unless you have actively opted in.

Thank you for reading this Privacy Policy. Your privacy is important, and this website is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
